VoxShift - voice changer, soundboard and Melody Voice (Windows)
===============================================================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example your Desktop).
2. Double-click VoxShift.exe.
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     (The app isn't signed by a company, so Windows warns about it.)
3. To be heard with the effects in Discord, games or OBS: install the free VB-CABLE driver
   from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ (run the setup as administrator, then reboot).
   VoxShift finds it by itself. In Discord, set your input device to "CABLE Output".
   Without VB-CABLE you can still try everything: pick your headphones as VoxShift's
   output and you hear yourself.
4. Pick a preset at the top of the Effects tab. For Melody Voice (talk, and it comes out
   sung): choose "Sing the Song (per-syllable)" and say a sentence slowly, one syllable at
   a time.

Got a .voxpack file from a friend? Click "Import..." next to the preset list (or drop the
file on the window): their presets and songs are added to yours.

Everything runs on your own PC: no account, no ads, nothing is uploaded.
